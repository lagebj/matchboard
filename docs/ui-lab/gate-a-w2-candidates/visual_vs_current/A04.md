# A04 — Evidence grammar: candidate vs current (XR-V01)

Per scenario: (1) coach question and source scope, (2) existing component/capability and
deficiency, (3) the candidate's visible change beyond colours/card shape, (4) comprehension/
workflow error prevented, (5) known limitations and accepted trade-offs.

## A04-S1 — Opportunity vs actual minutes

1. **Question:** did this player actually play in their eligible rounds, or only have the chance
   to? Scope: five eligible rounds, one player, one group.
2. **Current:** `PlayerParticipationStrip` (`src/components/touchline/player/player-
   participation-strip.tsx`) takes `minutes: number` and always renders a number — confirmed by
   direct read it has no path to render an absent measurement truthfully. `player-match-
   timeline.tsx` omits the minutes label entirely when `minutes` is null, which hides the absence
   rather than stating it. **`NO_BASELINE_CAPABILITY`** for this exact opportunity-vs-minutes
   shape.
3. **Candidate:** two separate panels — "5/5 eligible rounds with a recorded opportunity" (a real
   fact) and "Minutes: Not recorded" (an equally real, separately-coverage-stated fact) — shown
   together, never merged into one number or one confidence-sounding claim.
4. **Prevents:** a coach reading "5/5" alone and assuming it means five full games or five starts;
   prevents a missing-minutes measurement from silently defaulting to `0`, which would read as "no
   playing time" rather than "not measured".
5. **Limitations:** the fixture's `NOT_RECORDED` choice (over `UNKNOWN`) assumes the source can
   positively confirm no interval exists — a real source might need a third state for "we never
   checked" vs "checked, nothing there," which this fixture does not distinguish.

## A04-S2 — Sparse role exposure (2 of 6)

1. **Question:** is there enough recorded role exposure yet to say whether suitability is
   changing? Scope: six eligible matches, one role.
2. **Current:** `player-position-timeline.tsx` has its own "fewer than three entries" empty state,
   but it is scoped to that component's own display logic, not a 2-of-6-denominator shape with an
   explicit "no trend" answer. **`NO_BASELINE_CAPABILITY`**.
3. **Candidate:** states "2 of 6 eligible matches with recorded exposure" and an explicit "Not
   enough recorded matches to answer this question yet" — no sparkline, no growth arrow, no
   smoothed line, no inferred suitability change.
4. **Prevents:** a coach reading two data points as a trend (a real risk with any chart that
   connects points with a line) and drawing a suitability conclusion from scarce evidence.
5. **Limitations:** 2/6 is this fixture's proof of scarcity, not a universal "two is too few"
   product rule — a differently-shaped fixture could legitimately support a trend at a different
   ratio; this candidate does not decide that threshold.

## A04-S3 — Supported role exposure comparison (prior 3 vs latest 3)

1. **Question:** was recorded role exposure higher, lower, or unchanged across the six most
   recent complete matches? Scope: six eligible matches, one role, one time unit.
2. **Current:** no production surface currently partitions recorded role-duration minutes into a
   prior/latest window comparison with named denominators. **`NO_BASELINE_CAPABILITY`**.
3. **Candidate:** "Recorded CM exposure was higher in the latest three matches in this sample,"
   37 → 67 minutes, both windows' exact matches and minutes listed and inspectable — the
   conclusion wording is computed from the real totals (`direction`), never hard-coded.
4. **Prevents:** overclaiming causal/skill language ("faster development", "improving") from a
   purely descriptive interval comparison, and prevents treating six matches as a universal
   sufficiency threshold.
5. **Limitations:** the comparison says nothing about matches outside the sample, and nothing
   about why exposure changed (tactical choice, opponent, fitness, etc.) — deliberately out of
   scope.

## A04-S4 — Final score vs recorded event log

1. **Question:** what was the final score, and how complete is the recorded goal-by-goal
   timeline? Scope: one match, 6–4 final result.
2. **Current:** no production surface currently separates a canonical final score from an
   independently partial event log as two distinct coverage states in the same view.
   **`NO_BASELINE_CAPABILITY`**.
3. **Candidate:** "Final score: 6–4" (from the canonical result record, `COMPLETE`) shown
   alongside "1 individually logged goal event" (from the event log, `PARTIAL`) — two independent
   source classes, each with its own coverage badge, the score caption explicit that it is "not
   recomputed from the event log below".
4. **Prevents:** a coach assuming a 6–4 result implies ten fully-logged, reconstructable goal
   events, or a single logged event implies the match barely had any goals.
5. **Limitations:** this candidate does not attempt to backfill or estimate the other nine goals'
   timing/attribution from the final score — by design, per `02_A04_SCOPE_AND_FIXTURE_TRUTH.md`.

## A04-S5 — Central profile projection (LCM 20m + RCM 10m → CM)

1. **Question:** how much recorded CM-profile playing time does one match actually contribute
   when a player's minutes were split across two sided positions? Scope: one match, two intervals.
2. **Current:** W1's `a12-profile-editor` (APPROVED_GOLDEN) demonstrates the same aggregator
   across a two-match fixture; this exact single-match, non-overlapping-interval shape was not
   previously demonstrated.
3. **Candidate:** "CM: 30 recorded minutes, 1 distinct match" with both exact positions (LCM 20m,
   RCM 10m) still shown and inspectable in the drilldown, each with its own source record and an
   explicit named transformation ("Collapses into the CM profile group... for display only").
4. **Prevents:** reading 30 minutes as two matches, or reading the collapsed CM figure as if the
   exact sided evidence had been discarded.
5. **Limitations:** reuses W1's approved aggregator unmodified; the overlap-rejection invariant
   (new in this PR) is exercised by a positive case (this fixture) and a negative, test-only
   deliberately-overlapping variant — never rendered on the page itself.

## A04-S6 — Declared position vs actual evidence

1. **Question:** is a declared profile position the same thing as actual recorded playing
   experience? Scope: two players, same declared primary (CM).
2. **Current:** W1's `a12-profile-editor` (APPROVED_GOLDEN) already splits "Legacy declared
   positions" (Case A) from "Recorded match exposure" (Case B) for a *migration* preview; A04-S6
   extends the same declared-vs-evidenced distinction with S5's real non-overlapping-interval
   evidence for Case B, framed as the general evidence-grammar case rather than a legacy migration.
3. **Candidate:** Case A shows the declaration alone with an explicit "no actual match interval
   exists" caveat; Case B shows the same declaration next to the real recorded evidence (30
   minutes, 1 match) as two separate facts, with an explicit no-auto-promotion statement.
4. **Prevents:** reading a coach's declared position as proof of experience, and reading recorded
   evidence as if it silently became (or confirmed) the declaration.
5. **Limitations:** no production mutation path exists or is implied — selecting nothing here
   writes nothing; a real promotion from evidence to declaration still requires ADR-0139's
   confidence threshold and explicit coach approval, not simulated by this candidate.
