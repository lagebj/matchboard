# A12 — Profile picker & exact-match evidence boundary: candidate vs current

**Current:** `src/components/players/player-editor-form.tsx` (production) offers all 24 sided
`CanonicalTacticalPosition` codes for `primaryPosition`/`secondaryPosition`/`tertiaryPosition`,
via `src/lib/player-form-options.ts`. No 14-role picker, and no "collapsed profile vs exact
evidence" view exists anywhere today.

**Candidate:** `/dev/ui-lab/gate-a/a12-profile-editor` — a new, isolated 14-role
`ProfilePositionSelector` (visual only, writes nothing) next to a `LegacyEvidenceDrilldown`
panel that shows the real, un-hidden 24-code sided intervals behind the selected profile
(e.g. selecting `CM` shows `LCM — 20 min` and `RCM — 10 min`, summed to `30 min across 2
appearances`). The aggregation (`aggregateExactAppearancesByProfile`) is unit-tested to prove it
never drops, double-counts, or rewrites a sided interval.

**Delta from directional study:** Not pixel-compared (F1 study-02 not reviewed in detail this
wave).

**Usability rationale:** Makes the profile-vs-exact distinction legible and auditable in one
view — a coach sees the simplified label they declared and can still see exactly what match
evidence it's standing in for, without that evidence being silently merged or lost.
