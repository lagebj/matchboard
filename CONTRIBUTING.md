# Contributing to Matchboard

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Matchboard.

## How to contribute

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a feature branch from `main`.
3. Make your changes with clear, focused commits.
4. Open a pull request against `main`.

Use [Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/) style for commit messages.

## Contributor License Agreement

Substantive contributions require a Contributor License Agreement (CLA) to be accepted before the contribution can be merged.

**Current status:** The CLA is prepared but pending legal review. Substantive external contributions should not be merged until the CLA has been legally reviewed and activated. This status will be updated when the CLA becomes effective.

### What the CLA covers

The CLA grants Matchboard sufficient rights to use, modify, distribute, sublicense, relicense, and commercialize contributions as part of the Matchboard product, including under current and future licenses. Contributors retain ownership of their original contributions.

The CLA is not copyright assignment. Contributors retain the right to use their own contributions elsewhere.

### Trivial contributions

The following categories of changes are generally considered trivial and may not require a signed CLA:

- Typo fixes
- Formatting-only corrections
- Purely mechanical dependency version updates (e.g., Dependabot PRs)
- Insignificant documentation corrections

When in doubt, the CLA is required.

## Contribution rights and product governance

Accepting a contribution does not grant the contributor:

- Decision rights over the Matchboard product, roadmap, or architecture
- Veto power over future licensing or commercial decisions
- Ownership of any part of the Matchboard product beyond their original contribution

Matchboard maintainers retain final decision authority over:

- Roadmap and product direction
- Architecture and technical decisions
- Acceptance or rejection of contributions
- Modification or removal of contributed functionality
- Licensing and commercialization
- Dual licensing or relicensing

## Third-party code

Any third-party code included in a contribution must have licensing compatible with Elastic License 2.0 and must be clearly attributed. Do not copy code with unclear or incompatible licensing.

## Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive in all interactions.
