# Contributing to Matchboard

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Matchboard.

## How to contribute

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a feature branch from `main`.
3. Make your changes with clear, focused commits.
4. Open a pull request against `main`.

Use [Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/) style for commit messages.

## Contributor License Agreement

Substantive contributions require an accepted Contributor License Agreement (CLA) before merge. Opening or submitting a pull request is **not** implicit CLA acceptance.

### Active CLA

The Matchboard CLA is based on the [Harmony Project CLA](https://www.harmonyagreements.org/) v1.0 with **Option Five** (broad outbound licensing). It is **active** and enforced.

- **Individual CLA**: [matchboard-individual-cla.md](cla/matchboard-individual-cla.md)
- **Entity CLA**: [matchboard-entity-cla.md](cla/matchboard-entity-cla.md)

### What the CLA covers

The CLA grants Matchboard sufficient rights to use, modify, distribute, sublicense, relicense, and commercialize contributions as part of the Matchboard product — including under Elastic License 2.0, under future licenses, and under commercial or proprietary terms. Contributors retain ownership of their original contributions and can use their own contributions elsewhere.

The CLA is not copyright assignment.

### How to accept the CLA

Open an issue using the [CLA Acceptance template](https://github.com/lagebj/matchboard/issues/new?template=cla-acceptance.md). You will need to provide:

- Your GitHub username
- Your legal name (or entity name)
- CLA type (Individual or Entity)
- CLA version (currently 1.0)
- Explicit affirmative acceptance

Your acceptance will be recorded in `cla/cla-records.json` after review.

### CLA enforcement

A GitHub Actions workflow checks CLA status on pull requests from external contributors. Substantive PRs cannot be merged without an accepted CLA record.

### Individual and entity contributors

**Individual contributors**: You own or control the rights to the submitted work. Sign the Individual CLA.

**Entity/employer-owned contributions**: If your contribution is owned by your employer or another legal entity, either sign the Individual CLA with employer authorization or have the employer sign the Entity CLA. The process does not assume that a GitHub user personally owns all submitted IP.

### Trivial contributions

The following categories are generally considered trivial and may not require a signed CLA:

- Typo fixes
- Formatting-only corrections
- Purely mechanical dependency updates (e.g., Dependabot PRs)
- Insignificant documentation corrections

When uncertain, the CLA is required.

## Contribution rights and product governance

Accepting a contribution does not grant the contributor:

- Decision rights over the Matchboard product, roadmap, or architecture
- Veto power over future licensing or commercial decisions
- Ownership of any part of the Matchboard product beyond their original contribution

Matchboard maintainers retain final decision authority over:

- Roadmap and product direction
- Architecture and technical decisions
- Acceptance or rejection of contributions
- Modification or removal of contributed functionality
- Licensing and commercialization
- Dual licensing or relicensing

## Third-party code

Any third-party code included in a contribution must have licensing compatible with Elastic License 2.0 and must be clearly attributed. Do not copy code with unclear or incompatible licensing.

## Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive in all interactions.
