Matchboard is a coach-facing football operations tool for match-round squad planning,
controlled player movement, matchday responsibility, and post-match reflection across a
league season.

Start with [Getting started](/docs/getting-started) if you are new to Matchboard, or read
[Working with Matchboard](/docs/core-concepts/working-with-matchboard) to understand why
Matchboard is more useful the more of the coaching cycle you use it for.
