## What

Events cover cups, tournaments, and friendly days — occasions that need their own temporary
squads, built from your player pool, without touching league round selections.

## Why

A cup day squad is a different kind of decision than a normal league round: it usually needs a
one-off, event-specific balance of players rather than the season-long team structure your
league rounds already use. Keeping events separate means a tournament squad never creates a
league selection, never affects league fairness history, and never touches a finalized match.

## How

Create an event, mark player availability for it, and generate one or more squads: a single
competitive squad built around your strongest available line-up, a balanced split across
several squads, or squads seeded manually and balanced automatically around your locks. Squads
stay editable until you lock them; locking runs a validation pass first.

Matches within an event can share helpers across squads when their matches do not overlap in
time, similar to League helpers in normal rounds.

![Fjord Cup event overview showing a competitive squad and a development squad with balance summaries](/docs/screenshots/events/event-squads.png)

## Example

For the Fjord Cup, Fjordvik FK enters two squads. The coach generates one competitive squad
built around the strongest available line-up and lets the rest of the available players fill a
second, balanced squad automatically.

## What Matchboard does next

Event squads and matches get their own post-match reporting, separate from league statistics —
an event appearance is real playing time for the player, but it is not counted as a league
match.
