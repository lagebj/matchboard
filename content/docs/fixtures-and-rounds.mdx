## What

Fixtures is the one-stop view of your league season's structure: league season, then match
round, then individual match. It is also where you generate draft squads.

## Why

A match round is Matchboard's operational planning unit — one or more matches that share the
same week. Keeping the season, round, and match levels visible together, with actions at each
level, means a coach can move between "the whole season" and "this specific match" without
switching pages.

## How

- **Populate all** generates draft selections for every round whose planning boundary hasn't
  closed yet in the active league season, in one action.
- **Generate round** runs the same generation for a single round, considering every match in it
  together so player movement between matches in the same round is resolved consistently.
- There is no "Finalize" button. The plan becomes historical automatically the moment a match's
  real-world planning boundary closes — scheduled kickoff passes, or live match reporting
  starts, whichever happens first. Nothing needs locking by hand.

Each level shows its readiness state, a count of plan-integrity issues that need a decision,
and how many players are selected — not a raw warning count.

## Example

At the start of the week, Fjordvik FK's coach runs Populate all for the upcoming round, which
creates draft squads for Fjordvik Rød, Blå, and Hvit's matches at once. The coach then opens the
[Round Board](/docs/squad-planning) to review and adjust — the plan stays editable right up to
each match's own kickoff.

## What Matchboard does next

A round becoming historical does not mean its matches have been played — that is a separate
fact, shown once a match's post-match report is completed. If a match's kickoff time turns out to
be wrong and it genuinely hasn't started yet, rescheduling it reopens planning automatically. See
[Matchday](/docs/matchday) and [Post-match](/docs/post-match) for what happens once a match is
underway or complete.

See [Planning lifecycle](/docs/how-matchboard-works/planning-lifecycle) for exactly when and how
a round's planning boundary closes, and the narrow way a genuine reschedule reopens it.
