## What

Reports covers two things: exporting a season's finalized data, and reviewing the historical
record of rounds and matches that have already been finalized.

## Why

A season builds up a lot of planning detail — selections, movement, results. Coaches need to
get that data out for club reporting, and need a reliable way to look back at what was actually
decided and played without risking any accidental change to that history.

## How

**Season export** produces a coach or parent version of the season's data in your choice of
format. The coach version includes internal detail — roles, override reasons, plan-integrity
context. The parent version keeps to neutral, sharing-safe language and hides anything intended
only for coach planning.

**History** shows finalized rounds and matches exactly as they were locked. Historical data is
read-only from this page — a correction to a finalized report goes through the explicit
correction step described in [Post-match](/docs/post-match), never a silent edit here.

## Example

At the end of the season, Fjordvik FK's coach exports a parent-facing summary of each team's
rounds to share with families, and keeps the full coach version for their own season review.

## What Matchboard does next

Nothing in history changes on its own — a finalized round stays exactly as it was captured
unless a genuine reschedule proves a match didn't actually start and reopens its planning, or a
completed post-match report is deliberately reopened for correction (see
[Post-match](/docs/post-match)).
