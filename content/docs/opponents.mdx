## What

An opponent team is a reusable record shared across every match you play against them, holding
match history and short, factual notes about the environment of each encounter — never a rating
of the opposing team or its players.

## Why

Opponents recur across a season. Recording a short, factual note after each meeting — how
physical the match was, whether a Fair Play concern came up — builds a genuinely useful
encounter history instead of relying on memory before the next meeting.

## How

Each match links to a persisted opponent team. After the match, you can add a short factual
summary of the match environment; free text is checked to reject anything that looks like an
email address, phone number, or other identifying detail. A serious Fair Play concern is
recorded as a follow-up flag — the actual handling of a serious concern happens through your
club's normal process, outside Matchboard.

Opponent notes never change selection, fairness, or plan-integrity behaviour automatically —
they are context for the coach, not an input to the generation engine.

The opponent detail page separates three different kinds of evidence about the same team:

- **Estimated sporting level** — how strong the opponent has looked, based on results and the
  squad you fielded against them.
- **Tactical tendencies** — a repeated, observable style (for example, a high press) that only
  appears once a coach has recorded enough matching observations. A single observation shows as
  limited evidence, never as a confident tendency.
- **Combination evidence** — factual context about which of your own players have played
  together against this opponent, and for how long.

These are kept distinct on purpose: a strong opponent is not automatically assumed to play any
particular style, and a tactical tendency is never inferred from the scoreline alone.

## Example

After a physical match against Stormhavn IL, Fjordvik FK's coach adds a short note about the
pressing intensity Stormhavn showed. Before the next meeting, that note — alongside the
[opponent evidence history](/docs/evidence-and-learning) built from prior results — helps set
expectations for the upcoming match.

## What Matchboard does next

Every recorded encounter adds to that opponent's history automatically; nothing needs to be
re-entered for the next match against the same team.
