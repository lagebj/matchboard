## What

The Players page has three modes: a season overview of actual participation and match
statistics, current-round attention for a selected round, and base group management for
stable team assignment.

## Why

These are three different questions a coach asks at different times — "how has the season gone
for this player", "who needs a decision this round", and "which team does this player belong
to" — and mixing them into one view would make each harder to use. Keeping them separate keeps
each one specific to the question it answers.

## How

- **Season overview** shows played matches, goals, assists, and role counts (core, support,
  development) drawn from completed post-match reports — draft selections and finalized but
  unplayed matches are never counted as played. Sort and filter by team, load, or attendance
  pattern.
- **Current round attention** shows the live plan-integrity state for one round: which players
  are blocked, need a decision, or are fully covered.
- **Manage base groups** is for stable team belonging — assigning a player to their core team.
  It is separate from weekly selection: moving a player between matches for one round does not
  change their base group.

## Example

Reviewing Fjordvik Rød's season overview, the coach filters to players with high support load
to see who has been sent to help other teams most often this season, and checks whether that
load has been shared fairly across the available players.

## What Matchboard does next

Season statistics never drive selection or fairness scoring automatically — they are shown for
the coach to review, not an automatic verdict. See
[Evidence and learning](/docs/evidence-and-learning) for how observations recorded during and
after matches build into more useful context over time.
