## What

The Round Board is where you review a round's draft squads: who is selected for each match,
who has been sent as support or moved for development, and what still needs a decision.

## Why

Generated selections are a starting point, not a final answer. A coach may know something the
generation pass does not — a player is carpooling with a teammate, or should get a specific
challenge this week — and needs to see and adjust the plan with full visibility into why each
player was placed where they are.

## How

The board has one column of available players and one column per match. Dragging a player into
a match column selects them; the role — core, sent as support, or development — is worked out
automatically from their base team and any active rotation path, or you can choose it yourself
with a reason if no path exists.

Every selection has a "Why this selection" explanation: which rule placed them there, what
fairness or load impact it has, and whether it is a hard rule or a scoring preference. Plan
integrity is shown as **Blocked** conditions (must be resolved or consciously overridden before
finalizing), **Decision required** conditions (an available player has no match this round), and
**Planning notes** (useful context that does not block finalizing).

A match's own Tactics tab is where you choose its formation once the squad is settled:

![Match tactics tab with a suggested formation and alternative options](/docs/screenshots/planning/match-starting-plan.png)

## Example

Fjordvik Blå is short one player for its match against Havørn FK. The Round Board shows this as
a Blocked condition. The coach uses **Repair options** on a player chip to see a ranked list of
eligible replacements from teams with an active support path to Fjordvik Blå, and applies one.

## What Matchboard does next

Manual changes recalculate plan integrity, fairness impact, and the movement ledger immediately
— the board never shows a stale picture after an edit. Once the board is clear of unresolved
Blocked/Decision-required conditions (or you have consciously overridden them with a reason),
the round can be finalized from [Fixtures and rounds](/docs/fixtures-and-rounds).

The Round Board is fully usable on a phone for a quick check between matches, with the same
plan-integrity summary at the top:
