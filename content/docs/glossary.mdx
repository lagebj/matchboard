---
title: Glossary
description: Definitions for the terms Matchboard uses across planning, matchday, and evidence.
---

This glossary defines terms as Matchboard actually uses them, which sometimes differs from
everyday football usage — "rotation," in particular, means several related but distinct things
depending on context, and this glossary calls that out explicitly.

## Actual participation

What really happened in a match, as recorded during live reporting or confirmed in the post-match
report — who started, who came on, who moved position, and when. Distinct from the planned
squad: a player can be planned and not actually play, or play unplanned real minutes that were
never part of the plan. See [Matchday](/docs/matchday) and [Post-match](/docs/post-match).

## Availability

A player's current status for selection: Available, Tentative, Unavailable, Injured, Sick, Away,
or Unknown. Set by the coach on the Players page and read live by both generation and plan
integrity — there is no separate per-round history behind it, so a correction takes effect
immediately for any round that has not yet passed its planning boundary. See
[Fair playing opportunity](/docs/how-matchboard-works/fair-playing-opportunity).

## Combination evidence

Factual, descriptive context about how much time two or more players have shared on the pitch
together, and in what shape — never a compatibility score, and never a reason to permanently pair
or separate specific players. See [Evidence and learning](/docs/evidence-and-learning).

## Confidence

How much recorded history backs a piece of evidence — not a judgement about whether the pattern
is good or bad. Limited evidence and no evidence are both treated as neutral, never as a mark
against a player, position, or combination. See [Evidence and learning](/docs/evidence-and-learning).

## Core team

The team a player stably belongs to for the season. Belonging to a core team is separate from
which match a player is planned for in any given round — a player can be sent as support or moved
for development without changing their core team. See
[Squad selection engine](/docs/how-matchboard-works/squad-selection-engine).

## Development movement

A player moved to another team specifically for a development opportunity — a different or
harder context than their own core team currently offers — authorised by an active development
rotation path. Temporary and explainable, never a permanent reclassification. See
[Squad selection engine](/docs/how-matchboard-works/squad-selection-engine).

## Evidence

Factual context Matchboard builds up from completed matches — recorded minutes, positions,
combinations, opponent history, and more — that can influence later planning as a bounded
preference. Evidence never overrides fairness, availability, or eligibility, and missing evidence
is always treated as neutral. See [Evidence and learning](/docs/evidence-and-learning).

## Event

A cup, tournament, or friendly day: a self-contained occasion with its own temporary squads, kept
separate from league round planning. An Event does not create league selections, does not affect
league fairness history, and does not touch a finalized league match. See
[Events](/docs/events).

## Event squad

A named squad within one Event, distinct from a league Team — it exists only for that Event, has
its own target/min/max size, and does not carry season-long history the way a league core team
does. See [Events](/docs/events).

## Fairness

A scoring preference that helps Matchboard choose between reasonable options based on how
opportunity, support load, and development movement have been shared across a league season.
Fairness is subordinate to eligibility, availability, and a team's required support — it never
overrides them. See [Fair playing opportunity](/docs/how-matchboard-works/fair-playing-opportunity).

## Football observation

A short, coach-recorded note about a player's observable behaviour in a match — for example,
recovering position quickly, or helping a teammate after losing the ball — using a fixed set of
observable-behaviour codes, never a numeric grade or a character judgement. See
[Post-match](/docs/post-match).

## Guest Player

A reusable identity for someone taking part in a single Event or league round without becoming a
permanent member of your player group — a friend's child, or someone from another club. A Guest
Player behaves like a normal participant for the occasion they join, but never builds up
season-long statistics or development history the way a registered player does. See
[Events](/docs/events).

## Lineup

The starting formation and positions for one specific match, built from the match's selected
squad. A generated lineup is a starting point the coach can reassign slot by slot — never a claim
that it is the single strongest possible eleven.

## Match state

The canonical, reconstructed record of who was actually on the pitch, in what position, at any
point during a match — derived from the starting lineup plus every recorded substitution and
position change. Distinct from the plan: match state reflects what happened, not what was
intended. See [Matchday](/docs/matchday).

## Opponent level

An estimate of how strong an opponent has looked, based on results and the squad fielded against
them — one of several distinct kinds of evidence Matchboard keeps about an opponent, alongside
tactical tendencies and combination evidence. See [Opponents](/docs/opponents).

## Opportunity

Whether a player has a planned match to play in during a given round. An available, eligible
player without a planned opportunity is a condition Matchboard actively surfaces for a coach
decision, not something that passes unnoticed. See
[Fair playing opportunity](/docs/how-matchboard-works/fair-playing-opportunity).

## Plan integrity

The live-recomputed set of conditions Matchboard checks against a round's current plan: Blocked
conditions that need resolving, Decision required conditions that need a conscious choice, and
Planning notes that are useful context without blocking anything. Recomputed fresh on every read,
never left stale. See [Squad selection engine](/docs/how-matchboard-works/squad-selection-engine).

## Planned rotation

An intended position change or substitution for one specific match, set up before kickoff as part
of a whole-match rotation plan. Distinct from the movement described under "development movement"
or "support movement" above — a planned rotation is about what happens *during* one match, not
about which team a player belongs to. See [Rotations](/docs/rotations).

## Planning boundary

The real-world moment a match's plan becomes historical: its scheduled kickoff passing, or live
reporting starting for it, whichever happens first. There is no separate coach-operated finalize
action — the boundary closes automatically. See
[Planning lifecycle](/docs/how-matchboard-works/planning-lifecycle).

## Position experience

A player's actual recorded playing time in a given position, as distinct from their declared
primary, secondary, or tertiary position. Position experience can support considering a player for
a role they were not declared for, without permanently reclassifying them. See
[Players](/docs/players).

## Position-context evidence

A comparison of what has tended to happen while a specific player occupied a specific position
against the team's other recorded time in that same position — a descriptive pattern, never a
verdict on the player, and never on its own a reason to give a player fewer opportunities there.
See [Evidence and learning](/docs/evidence-and-learning).

## Role suitability

A factual read of how well-supported a given outfield role is for a player, from Natural (matches
declared primary position) through Plausible, Developmental, to Unsupported — never a permanent
label, and never applied to goalkeeping, which stays a direct coach decision. See
[Players](/docs/players).

## Rotation

An overloaded term Matchboard uses in three related but distinct senses. A **rotation path**
authorises movement of players between teams (support, development, or squad repair) — see
[Squad selection engine](/docs/how-matchboard-works/squad-selection-engine). A **planned
rotation** is an intended in-match position change or substitution — see
[Rotations](/docs/rotations). **Consecutive support rotation** describes how support load is
deprioritised for a player who carried it in the immediately preceding round or two, to spread it
across the squad over time. Context in each page makes clear which sense applies.

## Squad repair

Filling a gap left in a team's squad after support or development movement, in a defined priority
order that never uses a non-rotatable player. If no valid repair exists, Matchboard raises a
planning note rather than silently leaving the team short. See
[Squad selection engine](/docs/how-matchboard-works/squad-selection-engine).

## Support movement

A player sent from their core team to help another team reach a viable squad — the highest
generation priority after hard eligibility rules, resolved before any optional development
movement or fairness-driven rotation. See
[Fair playing opportunity](/docs/how-matchboard-works/fair-playing-opportunity).

## Target squad size

A team's planning aim for squad size — distinct from its minimum accepted size (a hard floor) and
its maximum size (a hard ceiling). A squad below target but above minimum is a planning note, not
a blocked condition. See
[Fair playing opportunity](/docs/how-matchboard-works/fair-playing-opportunity).
