These examples follow Fjordvik FK, a small three-team club used throughout this documentation,
through real planning decisions. Each example is generated from Matchboard's actual engines
against a real seeded dataset — not illustrative copy — so what you see here is what the product
actually produces, not a simplified retelling.

Each example links back to the deep concept page it demonstrates rather than re-explaining the
mechanism itself. Read the deep page first if a term here is unfamiliar; the
[glossary](/docs/glossary) also has short definitions for every term used across these examples.

## Available examples

- **[Why this squad?](/docs/examples/why-this-squad)** — how a round generated together resolves
  one team's shortfall using another team's surplus, and what still needs a coach decision
  afterward.
- **[Why this lineup?](/docs/examples/why-this-lineup)** — a real filled starting lineup, and why
  it is not a claim of the strongest possible eleven.
- **[Why this rotation?](/docs/examples/why-this-rotation)** — a planned position swap, the
  reactive evidence it surfaces before kickoff, and how the actual record differs from the plan.
- **[One match from plan to actual](/docs/examples/one-match-from-plan-to-actual)** — following
  one real match from the pre-match plan through matchday to the locked historical record it
  becomes.

More examples covering evidence, opponents, Events, and Guest Players are added to this section
as the corresponding deep pages are published.
