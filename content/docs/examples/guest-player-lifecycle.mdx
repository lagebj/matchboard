This example uses a real Guest Player added to Fjordvik FK's documentation dataset. See
[Guest Players](/docs/how-matchboard-works/guest-players) for the full reasoning before reading
this walkthrough.

## Initial conditions

Fjordvik FK is entering the Fjord Cup with a competitive squad and a balanced remainder squad,
built from the club's registered player pool. Kasper Lie, a friend of the club, is available to
help for this event only.

## What happens

Kasper Lie is created as a Guest Player with just a name, a source label ("Friend of the club"),
and a short note. He is added to the event's participant pool, marked available, and manually
assigned directly to the Fjordvik FK Development Squad.

![Fjordvik FK's Development Squad roster showing Kasper Lie labelled as a Guest alongside registered players](/docs/screenshots/events/guest-player-squad.png)

## Why

- A manual squad assignment for a Guest Player is respected immediately — there is no separate
  automatic-generation step to run, and no separate lock action needed to protect it.
- The squad's roster treats him identically to every registered player next to him for the
  purposes of this event: he counts toward the squad total, and the same position labels and
  squad-management controls apply.
- The "Guest" label is the one visible difference — never hidden, so a coach reviewing the squad
  always knows exactly who is a registered player and who is not.

## What this does not do

Nothing about Kasper Lie's participation touches Fjordvik FK's registered player statistics,
development history, or league fairness calculations — those exist only for players formally
added to the club's own player group. If Kasper Lie helps out again for a future event, the same
Guest Player identity is reused rather than recreated, but his participation still never becomes
part of any registered player's long-term record.

## What would change the outcome

If Fjordvik FK no longer expects Kasper Lie to help out, deactivating the Guest Player preserves
this event's historical participation exactly as it happened, rather than deleting it — there is
no delete action for a Guest Player with recorded history.
