This example uses a real generated lineup from Fjordvik FK's documentation dataset. See
[Lineup engine](/docs/how-matchboard-works/lineup-engine) for how the mechanism works before
reading this walkthrough.

## Initial conditions

Fjordvik Rød's squad for its match against Bergstad IF has 8 selected players, playing 7-a-side —
one bench spot. The `GK + 2-3-1` formation is chosen, with 7 slots: goalkeeper, two defensive
slots, three central slots, and one forward slot.

## What Matchboard generated

![Match tactics tab showing a filled 7-a-side lineup on the pitch, with the remaining squad listed alongside](/docs/screenshots/planning/generated-lineup.png)

Every slot is filled from the 8-player squad, goalkeeper first. Aksel Nord — the squad's only
goalkeeper-capable player — takes the goalkeeper slot without any comparison to anyone else. The
six outfield slots are then filled in order of how few compatible players remained for each one,
so a slot only one or two players could reasonably fill is decided before a slot several players
could take.

## Why

- Position fit dominates every other factor. A player with no declared or demonstrated fit for a
  slot is effectively never chosen for it.
- Scarcest slots are decided first specifically so a player who is the *only* reasonable fit for
  one slot is not accidentally used to fill a different slot that other players could equally
  well have taken.
- No attribute rating enters this decision at all. Two players who are both reasonable fits for a
  slot are treated as similarly reasonable, not ranked against each other by a skill number.

## What would change the outcome

A different formation would create a different set of slots and a different scarcity order. A
player marked unavailable, or one already locked into a different slot by the coach, would be
removed from consideration for the rest. Locking a slot directly — before or after this
suggestion runs — protects that specific placement from ever being reassigned by a later
suggestion.

## What this does not prove

This lineup is not a claim that it is the single strongest possible arrangement of these 8
players. It is one reasonable arrangement, built from position fit — a different formation, or a
coach's own manual reassignment, can produce an equally valid lineup. The player left on the
bench is not "worse" than the seven selected; a 7-a-side match only has seven starting places.
