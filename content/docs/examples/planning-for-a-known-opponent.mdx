title: "Worked example: Planning for a known opponent"
description: Four real recorded meetings between Fjordvik Rød and Bergstad IF, and what that history can and cannot tell a coach before the fifth.
---

This example uses Fjordvik FK's real recorded encounter history against Bergstad IF. See
[Opponent evidence](/docs/how-matchboard-works/evidence/opponent-evidence) for how each part of
this is built before reading this walkthrough.

## Initial conditions

Fjordvik FK has played Bergstad IF four times this league season, across two different Fjordvik
teams (Rød and Blå), with results ranging from a narrow loss to a clear win.

## What the opponent detail page shows

![Opponent detail page for Bergstad IF showing four recorded encounters, an estimated sporting level of 6.8 at high confidence, and no established tactical tendency yet](/docs/screenshots/evidence/opponent-evidence-history.png)

Four valid encounters back an estimated sporting level of 6.8, shown at high confidence — enough
recorded meetings for Matchboard to treat this as a reasonably settled estimate rather than a
first impression. Each contributing match is listed individually: date, score, and that match's
own estimate, ranging from 5.64 up to 7.62 across the four. Tactical tendencies show as "not
enough observations recorded yet" — a real, honest unknown, not a placeholder waiting to be
filled with an assumption.

## Why

- Four recorded meetings is enough for the sporting-level estimate specifically to reach high
  confidence — this evidence type's own threshold, distinct from the thresholds used for
  combination or match-phase evidence elsewhere in this documentation.
- Tactical tendency is separate evidence, built only from a coach's own recorded observations
  after a match — not inferred from these same four results. No observations exist yet, so no
  tendency is shown, honestly, rather than a tendency guessed from the scoreline.
- The four individual match estimates vary meaningfully (5.64 to 7.62) — a real spread, not a
  single fixed number pretending to be more precise than the evidence supports.

## What this does not prove

Four matches is a real, usable sample for this specific evidence type, but it is still four
matches, not a law about how Bergstad IF will play. The estimate describes how fixtures against
this opponent have tended to compare so far — it does not predict the result of a fifth meeting,
and it says nothing at all about tactical style, since that requires its own, separate evidence.

## What would change the picture

A coach recording match-environment observations after future meetings would eventually let a
real tactical tendency emerge, following the same confidence rules covered in
[Confidence and guardrails](/docs/how-matchboard-works/evidence/confidence-and-guardrails). A
fifth result — however it goes — adds one more data point to the sporting-level estimate rather
than replacing what the first four already established.
