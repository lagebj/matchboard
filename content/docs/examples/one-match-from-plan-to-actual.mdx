---
title: "Worked example: One match from plan to actual"
description: Following Fjordvik Rød's match against Bergstad IF from the pre-match plan through to the completed, locked historical record.
---

This example follows one real match in Fjordvik FK's documentation dataset through every stage
covered separately elsewhere in this documentation: [squad selection](/docs/how-matchboard-works/squad-selection-engine),
[lineup](/docs/how-matchboard-works/lineup-engine), [rotation planning](/docs/how-matchboard-works/rotation-engine),
[matchday](/docs/matchday), and [post-match reporting](/docs/post-match). Each stage is real, seeded
data, not a narrative summary.

## Before kickoff

Fjordvik Rød's squad for the match against Bergstad IF is generated for the round, a 7-a-side
`GK + 2-3-1` starting lineup is set with Elias Storm centrally and Theo Falk wide, and one planned
rotation is built: swap Elias and Theo's positions around the 30-minute mark. See
[Worked example: Why this lineup?](/docs/examples/why-this-lineup) and
[Worked example: Why this rotation?](/docs/examples/why-this-rotation) for these two stages in
full.

## During the match

The coach applies the planned swap at minute 33 — three minutes later than planned, recorded
honestly rather than adjusted to fit the plan. Fjordvik Rød score three: Elias Storm in the 12th
minute, Theo Falk in the 41st (with an assist from Elias Storm), and one from an opposition own
goal in the 55th. Bergstad IF score once. The match finishes 3–1.

## After the match

Ending live reporting seeds the post-match report directly from what was recorded — attendance,
goals, assists, and the position change do not need to be re-entered. The coach adds one football
observation: that Theo Falk adjusted well to the wide role after the swap, kept combining with
teammates rather than forcing it alone. Completing the report locks the match's record.

## What this becomes

Once locked, this match becomes part of Fjordvik FK's comparable history: minutes and positions
feed [position experience](/docs/how-matchboard-works/role-suitability-and-position-experience),
the shared time between Elias Storm and Theo Falk feeds combination evidence, the result and
match environment feed Bergstad IF's [opponent history](/docs/opponents), and the recorded
observation becomes part of Theo Falk's development record. None of this required re-entering
anything that was already captured earlier in the same match's lifecycle.

## What this does not prove

One match is one data point. The pattern shown while planning the rotation earlier — Fjordvik
Rød's record in this phase of matches — already existed before this match added to it, and this
one result does not settle anything on its own. See
[Evidence and learning](/docs/evidence-and-learning) for what a season of matches recorded this
way is actually useful for.
