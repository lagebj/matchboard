## What

Settings and access covers your organisation's membership: who has access, what role they have,
and how new coaches are invited in.

## Why

Matchboard holds real planning detail about real children, so access is deliberately
invitation-only rather than open sign-up — a new organisation is provisioned by the Matchboard
maintainer, and new members join an existing organisation only by accepted invitation.

## How

Roles determine what a member can do: full coaching access, admin-level membership management,
or a read-only viewer role for someone who should be able to see plans without editing them.
Invitations are sent by email and accepted explicitly — opening a link is not enough on its own
to gain access. An invitation can be revoked before it is accepted.

## Example

Fjordvik FK's head coach invites an assistant coach with full coaching access, and invites a
committee member with the read-only viewer role so they can follow round planning without being
able to change it.

## What Matchboard does next

Membership and role changes take effect immediately — a revoked member loses access without
needing to sign out and back in.
