## What

Today is the page Matchboard opens to. It shows the next action based on where your league
season currently stands, along with setup progress and anything that needs a decision before
you can move on.

## Why

A coach opening Matchboard should not have to work out for themselves what to do next. Today
derives that from your actual current data — draft rounds waiting to be reviewed, unresolved
plan-integrity issues, incomplete post-match reports — rather than a static checklist.

## How

Today surfaces items in priority order: blocked or decision-required plan issues first, then
setup gaps, then routine upcoming work. Selecting an item takes you straight to the page where
you can act on it — a round that needs review, a match missing a report, or a squad that still
needs players.

Today does not show planning notes, seasonal fairness commentary, or long-term coaching intent
— those live on [Fixtures and rounds](/docs/fixtures-and-rounds) and the team pages, where they
have the fuller context to be useful.

![Today page showing a blocked-conditions next action and event work items](/docs/screenshots/getting-started/today-overview.png)

## Example

On the morning of the round, Fjordvik FK's coach opens Matchboard and sees that Fjordvik Blå's
match against Havørn FK is short of players and needs a decision before the round can be
finalized. Selecting it opens the round directly at the match in question.

## What Matchboard does next

Once you act on an item — resolving a plan issue, completing a report — Today's list updates to
reflect the new state; it does not need to be refreshed by hand.
