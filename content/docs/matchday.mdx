## What

Live reporting is a real-time matchday screen for recording the score, match clock, goals,
assists, and substitutions as the match happens, plus a read-only "Follow live" view a second
coach can open without any recording controls.

## Why

Recording events as they happen is more accurate than reconstructing them afterwards, and it is
what makes the actual account of the match — not just the plan — available to
[post-match reporting](/docs/post-match) and later history. See
[Working with Matchboard](/docs/core-concepts/working-with-matchboard) for why this matters
beyond the day itself.

## How

Before kickoff, the pre-match package shows the current squad plan, starting lineup, and any
League helpers added for the match — right up to the moment kickoff passes or live reporting
starts, whichever happens first, when that plan becomes the captured historical baseline.
During the match, the reporting coach records goals,
assists, substitutions, and position changes against a running clock. A second coach with
access to the same team can open **Follow live** to watch the same events update in real time —
it never exposes any recording control, only the score, clock, and event stream.

## Example

During Fjordvik Rød's match against Bergstad IF, the coach records a goal for Elias Storm in
the 34th minute, then a planned rotation change at half-time. A parent-side assistant coach
opens Follow live on their own device to watch the score update without being able to record
anything themselves.

## What Matchboard does next

Ending the live session seeds the [post-match report](/docs/post-match) directly from what was
recorded — attendance, goals, assists, and position history do not need to be re-entered by
hand.

Live reporting is deliberately kept to facts and execution: the score, the clock, and what
actually happened. The historical patterns, evidence, and generated-plan reasoning described in
[Evidence and learning](/docs/evidence-and-learning) and [Rotations](/docs/rotations) belong to
planning before the match and reflection after it — showing that kind of analysis during a live
match would be a distraction, not a help, at the moment a coach is trying to run the game.
Recording the match accurately here is what makes that later planning and reflection possible in
the first place.
