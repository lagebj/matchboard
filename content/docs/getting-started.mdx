## What

Getting started with Matchboard means adding the setup data a league season needs: teams,
players, and matches. Once that exists, Matchboard can generate draft squad selections for
you.

## Why

Matchboard plans squads for matches that already exist — it does not create fixtures or
schedule a season for you. Setup is a one-time (or per-season) data-entry step that unlocks
the planning workflow described in [Working with Matchboard](/docs/core-concepts/working-with-matchboard).

## How

1. **Add teams** — each team belongs to your organisation and has a target/minimum/maximum
   squad size and a support-priority rank. See [Fixtures and rounds](/docs/fixtures-and-rounds)
   for how teams relate to rounds and matches.
2. **Add players** — assign each player to a core team. See [Players](/docs/players) for the
   season overview and base-group management.
3. **Add matches** — record each match's opponent, date, home/away, and format. Matches are
   grouped into rounds automatically by week.
4. **Mark availability** — before generating a round, mark which players are available.
5. **Populate all** — generate draft selections for every non-finalized round in one action.
   See [Squad planning](/docs/squad-planning) for what happens next.

## Example

Fjordvik FK's coach adds three core teams (Fjordvik Rød, Fjordvik Blå, Fjordvik Hvit), the
club's player roster, and the first round of league matches against Stormhavn IL, Solsiden SK,
and Skogheim FK. Running Populate all generates a first draft squad for every match in that
round.

## What Matchboard does next

Once draft selections exist, the [Squad planning](/docs/squad-planning) and
[Fixtures and rounds](/docs/fixtures-and-rounds) pages become the working surfaces for
reviewing, adjusting, and finalizing each round.
